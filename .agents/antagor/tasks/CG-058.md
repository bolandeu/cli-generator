Antagor lets the Architect of a project commit a hand fix with no task: no new behaviour and no test change, at most 2 existing text or config files (Markdown, TOML, YAML, the CI folders) and 10 changed lines, no path an active task owns. Antagor's pre-commit hook checks it, journals it, and it goes out with the next `antagor publish --main`. The bounds are `[architect.hand_fix]` in the Antagor config. A generated package's rules say when a change is a hand fix and when it is a task.

1. The generated AGENTS.md has a short rule: a hand fix is for the smallest change within those bounds; code, a test, or a change to what a rule means is always a task.
2. The generated CODING_STANDARDS.md has the same line where it lists what a change must pass, so a reviewer sees it.
3. Standard, Release gets one Recommended line with the same rule.
4. Nothing in the skill's update part: it is Antagor's default.

## Basis

Follow the style of the files: the generated AGENTS.md and CODING_STANDARDS.md are in Russian, the Standard in English; Standard sections are cited by name. No mention of our own ADRs, plans, ticket or idea ids, or dates (the no-dev-docs test checks it).

## Out of scope

Antagor's hook or config, the bounds themselves, existing packages' docs, the gate.
