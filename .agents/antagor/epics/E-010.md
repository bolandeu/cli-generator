## Problem

docs/plans/freeze-list.md and docs/plans/simple-standard.md are plans kept as files: news 6 (the Human 28.10) says a plan is not a file, and what it puts off becomes an idea, one per item. Until they are sorted, the leftovers are invisible to idea triage and to the epic that would take them.

## Goal

docs/plans/ is empty: every put-off item of the freeze list and every gap left by the simple-standard plan is an idea in this repository's list, with a number the Human can see, and no plan file duplicates the Standard.

## Acceptance (Given–When–Then)

- Given the two plan files, when the task is done, then every put-off item and finding is an idea in , and each file's sections map to those numbers in the task report
- Given 2 docs to sort:
  docs/plans/freeze-list.md
  docs/plans/simple-standard.md
ways out:
  - `antagor idea add` (a plan, backlog or brief is an idea or a task, not a file)
  - an ADR: docs/adr/NNNN-slug.md
  - a research record: docs/research/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md
  - a living doc: a name or folder from the default list, or `[docs] living` in antagor.toml
  - `[docs] data` or `[docs] generated` in antagor.toml for product data or generated files, when the task is done, then it names no .md to sort

## Implementation decisions

- The plan files go only after the Human says yes on their extracted items; until then the task keeps them in place.

- implementing any of the extracted items
- dropping a plan without the Human's yes on its items
