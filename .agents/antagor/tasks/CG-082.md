From news 6 (the Human 28.10): a plan is not a file. Two are left, and only they.

`docs/plans/freeze-list.md` — 16 bullets written during the v1.26.0 freeze: findings from the update batches 2-5, the gate and CI notes, the parallel-work incident.
`docs/plans/simple-standard.md` — the plan behind E-005/E-006/E-007, mostly shipped.

Do this, and nothing else:

1. For each bullet of `freeze-list.md` decide, by checking the current code, the Standard and the gate, one of:
   - `open` — still true today, and the fix is ours (one line: what to do);
   - `done` — shipped since (name the commit, the rule or the epic);
   - `elsewhere` — belongs to another product (`sysadmin`, `antagor`, a package), one line who;
   - `dropped` — no longer worth doing, and why.
2. Same for `simple-standard.md`: one line per decision — where it lives now (the Standard section, an ADR, an epic).
3. Report a table: section or bullet -> verdict -> the idea title it would get. No prefixes, no numbers: this report is what the Architect turns into `antagor idea add` calls on main.

Two things this task must not do:
- Do not write into `.agents/antagor/ideas/`: Antagor's own files do not merge from a worktree, the change is lost.
- Do not delete either file: a plan goes only with the Human's yes on the extracted items.

`antagor layout check` stays red until those two files are gone — that is expected and is not a finding.
